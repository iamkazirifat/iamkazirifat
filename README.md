## How I work

Before writing anything, I spend time in Reddit threads and forums reading what your audience actually says — their exact words, their questions, their frustration.

Then I write for that person. Not a demographic. A specific reader with a specific problem.

My process, in order:

1. Research the audience before touching the brief
2. Build an outline — what goes where, and why
3. Write sections first, intro last (always)
4. Edit to cut, not to polish
5. Add a table of contents so readers can find what they need

## Things I won't write

Crypto. Blockchain. Web3. Sports. Step-by-step technical guides that require me to perform the steps myself.

The rule isn't moral — it's practical. I only write what I can verify. Credibility is the line.

---

## What separates working content from content that just exists

No hype in the opening. No assertions without evidence. No data older than 5 years.

If a piece doesn't give the reader a clear next step, it hasn't finished its job.
