## How I Work

Before I write anything, I research the audience. Not the brief. The audience.

I go to forums and Reddit to read what real people actually say about the topic: their exact words, their frustrations, the questions they ask when they think no one is watching. Then I write for that person specifically.

My process, in order:

1. Understand the audience before touching the outline
2. Build an information checklist before writing begins
3. Write sections first, introduction last
4. Edit to cut and clarify, not to polish
5. Add a table of contents so both skim readers and full readers get what they need

I do not write what I cannot verify. If I cannot find enough evidence to stand behind a claim, I do not make it. That is the line between credibility and filler.

## What I Will Not Write
